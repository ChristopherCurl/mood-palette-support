Frequently asked questions

What is The Mood Palette?

One app with two modes. In kid mode, children check in with how they feel through play — a living color ring, a tiny companion, and calm-down tools. In parent mode, parents get a simple, non-invasive window into their child's emotional world, plus safety features like SOS with live location.

How do the two modes work?

Download the app once and choose your mode at setup. A family account links a parent's phone to their child's. Kids check in; parents see the ring.

Is it spying on my child?

No. The Mood Palette is non-invasive by design: no message reading, no tracking beyond what matters. Kids check in because they want to. Raw health signals never leave your child's device — only the color of the moment is shared.

What does SOS do?

SOS alerts a parent immediately and streams the child's live location. Parents can send a one-tap text to 911 with the live location attached. Nothing is ever sent automatically — the parent always stays in control.

How much does it cost?

Free for one child. $99/year covers the whole family — every child, every feature.

Is my family's data safe?

Yes. No ads, no sale of your data — ever. Children's data is never sold or shared with third parties. Read our Privacy Policy.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

Yes — the Mood Palette Watch app lets kids check in from their wrist, with SOS one press away.
