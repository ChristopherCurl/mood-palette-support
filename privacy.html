Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Your family's feelings are yours. We don't sell data, we don't show ads, and raw health signals never leave your child's device.

What we collect

  • Account info you provide (name, email)
  • Your child's check-ins (emotion + time) — shared with the parent account only
  • Live location only during an active SOS, streamed to the parent and emergency contacts you choose
  • Device health signals (heart rate, sleep, activity) are processed on-device to derive wellness context. Raw values never leave the device.

What we never do

  • No ads. No third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No sale of personal data, ever.
  • No sale or sharing of children's data with third parties.
  • Children's data is never used for marketing.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

The child mode of the app is directed to children under 13 and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. A parent creates the family account and provides verifiable parental consent before a child profile is created. Parents can review, pause, or delete their child's data at any time from parent mode.

Data retention

Check-in history is kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of account deletion. SOS location streams expire automatically (2-hour links).

Your rights

Access, correct, or delete your family's data anytime from the app or by contacting us at curlprivatecapital@gmail.com.
